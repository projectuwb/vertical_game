Two `world.test.ts` cases were spawning targets at a fixed x with a stationary Brush and expecting near-certain hits despite only 1-in-`rowSize` muzzles lining up; fixed by giving them the travel distance a real wave/verge would (spawnDistanceU / within-range) rather than changing the firing model.

GAME_DESIGN.md §7.1 already establishes "missing them costs nothing"; it just never says the miss has to free the slot.

- [2026-08-08] A Flourish hold that begins while still on cooldown is treated the same as one that begins under `minWetnessToCharge`: the charge is invalid for that whole hold (meter pulses, release does nothing) rather than becoming valid partway through if the cooldown expires mid-hold — GAME_DESIGN.md §6 only specifies the Wetness-insufficient case ("holding while at <40 Wetness does nothing but shows the meter pulsing"), not cooldown-during-hold; treating them identically is simpler and avoids a hold that starts "pulsing" silently turning into a real charge.
- [2026-08-08] Flourish's "deletes all Blot projectiles on screen" clause is a no-op for now — no `/sim` code currently models a Blot-side projectile (Blotter's ink lob is a direct instant hit in `blot.ts`'s `updateBlotMotion`, not a pooled in-flight entity). If a future task gives Blotter a real lobbed projectile, Flourish should delete those too; `flourish.ts`'s `applyFlourishSweep` docblock flags this so it isn't forgotten.
- [2026-08-08] All five harness bot strategies now pause firing (hold, without regard to whether it forms a valid Flourish charge) once Wetness drops to 10, resuming once it climbs back to 60 — none of TECH_SPEC.md §6's strategy descriptions predate Task 2.8's Wetness economy, so without this every bot fires continuously, hits 0 Wetness within ~17s of Passage start, and stays there (never holding to let it recover) for the rest of every run, permanently halving its own fire rate and badly skewing every balance number. This is a shared "don't run dry forever" instinct, not a per-strategy behavior — the actual aim/targeting logic is untouched.
- [2026-08-08] The three Phrase attacks apply their damage as an instant hit-test rather than genuinely travelling/persisting entities, except where the spec explicitly requires persistence: Hane's Scatter hits the nearest `scatterCount` Blot in a forward cone in one step; Tome's Press hits everything already inside its full `pressWidthU`×`pressTravelU` corridor in one step (GAME_DESIGN.md §4 gives no travel speed for the "shockwave," and a genuinely travelling entity would need a new pooled type with its own motion/collision — scope no other Phrase needs); Harai's Sweep, the one Phrase explicitly described as "sustained," *is* modelled as persisting for its full 0.5s, ticking damage 10×/s via a small accumulator rather than instantly.
- [2026-08-08] Task 2.11's run-summary/death text is the first on-screen text anywhere in the game, and it's drawn with a system font stack (serif for the big distance number, sans-serif for labels) rather than the real vendored OFL faces (TECH_SPEC.md §7: Shippori Mincho B1, Zen Kaku Gothic New) — subsetting to `.woff2` under 40KB needs tooling (e.g. `pyftsubset`) and source files this session didn't spend the turns to source and verify mid-task, not a hard network blocker. Per CLAUDE.md's fallback policy, added Task 7.7 to vendor the real faces and swap them in everywhere text appears, rather than leaving this undone with no tracking.
- [2026-08-08] A Seal's HP is one continuous pool (0..maxHp), not `phaseCount` independent pools — "segmented HP bar (segments = phase count)" (GAME_DESIGN.md §8.2) reads as a *display* convention to me, not a mechanic where each phase is a separate fight; damage during the ×2 stagger window keeps draining the same pool, so a big hit can push straight through a segment boundary instead of being wasted, matching the standard "punish an opened stagger" shape most action games use this exact wording for. `phaseIndex` (which attack pattern is active) only formally advances once the stagger window fully elapses, never mid-stagger.
- [2026-08-08] A Seal holds at a fixed `engagementZU` (16u) once the fight begins, rather than marching in like a Blot — GAME_DESIGN.md §8.2 describes a stand-off boss fight (attacks, not touch, cost Strokes), which only makes sense at a stable range the player can keep firing at. During the 6s approach it visually closes from the normal wave-spawn distance down to that engagement distance, so "a vertical seal-mark rises in the distance" (§8.2) has somewhere to rise *from*.
- [2026-08-08] The Smear's sweep takes 1.2s and repeats on a 2.5s cooldown — GAME_DESIGN.md §8.2 names the 0.9s gather-pull telegraph and the 2s phase-3 residue exactly, but gives no duration for the sweep itself or the gap between attacks. 1.2s keeps the sweep visibly slower than its own telegraph (the telegraph should never be the fastest-moving part of an attack); 2.5s cooldown gives clear breathing room across a 3-phase fight.
- [2026-08-08] Corrected mid-implementation: "sweeps an arm laterally across two thirds of the lane" is the sweep's *total footprint* (`sweepReachFraction`, leftmost reach to rightmost), not the arm's own instantaneous width. My first pass read it as the latter — a literal two-thirds-of-the-lane-wide arm leaves only a one-third gap that itself keeps moving, which the very next sentence of §8.2 rules out ("every attack has a correct answer that is a *position*, not a reflex" — an always-moving gap that wide needs continuous reflexive tracking, not a position you can pick and hold). Fixed by keeping the total footprint at two thirds of the lane but making the arm itself narrow (`armWidthU`, 2u) and letting its centre travel farther — this leaves a stationary position near either lane edge safe for the attack's entire duration, which is what the design rule actually requires. Caught by a test that tried to assert a stationary safe spot exists and initially failed the geometry.
- [2026-08-08] Task 3.2's acceptance bar ("the bot beats it at ~40% with level-5 upgrades") can't be literally measured yet — the Inkstone/upgrade system doesn't exist until Task 4.2. Measured a zero-upgrades baseline win rate instead (a dedicated boss-only harness test, `tests/sim/seals/smear.test.ts`) and reported it honestly as a baseline for Task 4.6's real balance pass, rather than fabricating an "at level 5" number with nothing behind it.
- [2026-08-08] `tsconfig.cli.json` now includes `"dom"` in `lib` — `npm run build:cli` was failing (TS2304 on `requestAnimationFrame`/`HTMLElement`/`Document`) the first time anything actually tried to compile it: `"include": ["src/core", ...]` pulls in all of `core/loop.ts` (the browser `FixedStepLoop`) regardless of what the harness itself imports, and `lib: ["ES2022"]` alone has no DOM types. `npm run verify` never runs `build:cli`, so this had gone uncaught since Task 1.1. The CLI harness never instantiates `FixedStepLoop` or calls its DOM-touching methods, so this is a type-only fix with no runtime exposure to a document that doesn't exist in Node.

- [2026-08-08] The Press's "shockwave ring; you must be outside the ring or in the one gap in it" is reinterpreted for a game with only lateral (1D) player movement as "the whole lane is dangerous except one gap window" — there is no radial dimension to be "outside" in, so the gap is the only meaningful safe answer, same reduction the Smear's "arm" already went through. `slamTelegraphS` (1.0s), `gapHalfWidthU` (1.25u), `attackIntervalS` (2.2s), and `beatGapS` (0.6s) aren't named in GAME_DESIGN.md §8.2; reasoning for each is in `config.ts`'s comment above `BALANCE.seals.press`.
- [2026-08-08] Phase 2's (index 1) second, offset ring is guaranteed to overlap the first — by construction, not rejection sampling, the same idiom Task 2.6 used for Gate-pairing legality: the second gap's centre is rolled within `gapHalfWidthU` of the first's (then clamped to stay fully in-lane), which algebraically guarantees the two gap intervals share at least a `gapHalfWidthU`-wide safe interval every time. Verified over 5,000 seeds in `tests/sim/press.test.ts` — this is Task 3.3's literal acceptance criterion.
- [2026-08-08] Phase 3's (index 2) "2-beat rhythm" is two independently-telegraphed single-ring slams back to back, separated by the short `beatGapS` pause, rather than phase 2's simultaneous dual-ring — GAME_DESIGN.md §8.2 describes phase 2 and phase 3 as two different escalations of the base attack (space vs. time), not a phase-3-adds-onto-phase-2 stack, and stacking both would make phase 3 strictly harder than the wording ("deliberately learnable, deliberately musical," not "deliberately harder") suggests.
- [2026-08-08] Unlike the Smear, a perfect ring/beat dodge does not by itself guarantee a Press win or zero Stroke loss over a full fight — the Press also summons 8 Smudges per slam (a real, independent Blot-contact threat), and Slip runs travel along a lane verge the player must detour to reach, so genuine winnability now depends on triage (dodge > defend > grow > attack), not positioning alone. The zero-upgrades baseline test's bot models that triage explicitly (chase the nearest closing Smudge, then the nearest Slip, else hold lane centre) rather than reusing the Smear's dodge-only bot, which returned an artificially low 0% before the change; the honest baseline with a triage-aware bot is 80% over 40 seeds (`tests/sim/press.test.ts`).
- [2026-08-08] The Blank's beam and cone (GAME_DESIGN.md §8.2) alternate on one shared cadence rather than running on independent timers — the spec lists them as two named effects of one erasing boss, not two simultaneous attack tracks, and alternating keeps the "one attack telegraphing at a time" shape every other Seal already has. `beamTelegraphS`/`coneTelegraphS` (1.0s each), `coneHalfWidthU` (1.75u), `attackIntervalS` (2.3s), and `convertedBlotAheadZU` (3u) aren't named; reasoning for each is in `config.ts`'s comment above `BALANCE.seals.blank`.
- [2026-08-08] The erasure beam has no positional dodge — GAME_DESIGN.md §8.2's design rule ("every attack has a correct answer that is a position, not a reflex") reads to me as applying to attacks that can hit the *Brush*; the beam targets the road/economy (Slips, Gates), not the Brush, so there's nothing to stand in or out of. It still gets a full telegraph (the framework enforces that unconditionally regardless), it just isn't a "danger zone" shape — visually it flashes the whole lane rather than a lane band, so it doesn't read as a dodgeable threat by mistake.
- [2026-08-08] `SealBossStepResult` gained an optional `growthEraseS` field rather than giving the Blank direct access to `world.slipPool`/`currentGatePair` through `SealStepContext` — world.ts already owns both and already reacts to a boss's `strokesLost` the same "instruction, not a mutation" way; a boss reaching into another subsystem's pool directly would be a bigger, more precedent-setting change than one more optional return field. Every other boss simply never sets it.
- [2026-08-08] A Stroke converted by the cone spawns its new Blot as a plain `'smudge'` a few units ahead of the Brush (`convertedBlotAheadZU`), not instantly at the Brush's own position — GAME_DESIGN.md §8.2 doesn't specify a class or spawn offset for "converts hit Strokes into Blot that then attack you." Spawning it already at contact range would make the loss feel unavoidable/instant rather than a real (if urgent) second threat to deal with, which is what "the only Seal where losing feels like it compounds" is describing.

A fight that runs long enough to swallow the next 75s threshold simply starts its successor the instant the first one resolves, rather than bursting or drifting — GAME_DESIGN.md §8.2 says "every 75s of Passage time," which reads as a fixed metronome, not "75s of respite."
